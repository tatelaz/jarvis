What stays on your PC and what leaves it

Jarvis works on your PC. Speech recognition, Jarvis's voice, memory and the voices it knows stay here: audio never leaves the PC.

Only these leave it:

- the text of what you ask, sent to the brain you choose at first start (Anthropic for Claude, OpenCode for the free models; nothing with the local model);
- web searches and the things you ask it to do online;
- WhatsApp and Gmail, if you connect them, talk to their own services;
- once a day a question to GitHub to check for a new version (turn it off by saying "updates off");
- at first start, the model downloads from GitHub and Hugging Face;
- reports to the developers, only if you send them or, after a crash, if you answer yes: a technical report with no sentences from your conversations, names, emails or numbers. After a crash they can be turned off by saying "crash reports never".

No ads and no usage statistics. Keys and app passwords are saved encrypted by Windows, readable only by your user. Your data stays in the Jarvis folder, under data.

Jarvis is open source (Apache 2.0 license): https://github.com/tatelaz/jarvis
